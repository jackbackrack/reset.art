reset.art

Reset expands access to contemporary art by equipping artists, gallerists, and organizers to build spaces of their own — sharing the tools, knowledge, and networks that make independent art communities possible.

Our mission is to expand access to contemporary art by building shared infrastructure — including tools, knowledge, peer networks, and organizational frameworks — that enables artists, gallerists, and community organizers to create, present, and sustain new work in spaces they initiate and operate. Reset pursues this mission through exhibitions, curatorial projects, public programs, artist development, and the replication of scalable models that support the formation of independent art spaces and communities, with the goal of increasing the geographic and demographic reach of contemporary art in the United States.
